the detail page of the programme that suits them. Release 1 has no visible
filters and does not display prices in the catalogue.

## 4. Programme Card Content Model

Each card uses the following fields:

- enrolment status badge;
- Programme Area;
- Programme Type;
- programme name;
- short value description;
- duration and learning volume;
- format;
- language of instruction;
- document summary;
- `View programme` CTA.

Programme Area and Programme Type link to their respective SEO landing pages.
The card itself leads to the programme detail page.

## 5. Enrolment Status Labels

Approved public labels:

- `Ongoing enrolment` — continuously available programme;
- `Enrolment open` — enrolment is open for a specific cohort;
- `Coming soon` — programme is published before enrolment opens;
- `Enrolment closed` — programme remains visible but does not currently accept
  applications.

Status rules:

- status is not inferred from the page locale;
- `Coming soon` is not used because a programme is taught in another language;
- time-sensitive status and start-date text must come from the current programme
  run or an admin-approved override;
- status is never communicated by colour alone.

## 8. Catalogue Rules

- no visible filters in Release 1;
- no current or previous prices on catalogue cards;
- no `Coming soon` status based only on presentation or instruction language;
- programme facts come from the approved programme record and localized master
  copy, not from independent catalogue-only text;
- hidden or unpublished programmes do not appear in the public catalogue;
- all cards use equivalent factual content in EN, UA, and CZ;
- cards must not imply that EN or CZ page localization means instruction in that
  language.

## 9. Publication Dependencies

- keep programme-run statuses and the Neuroplastic Reconstruction start date
  current;
- complete native-language review;
- prepare programme-specific card and Open Graph visuals with approved alt text.
