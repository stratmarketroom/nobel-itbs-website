## 1. Editorial Role

The Home page introduces Nobel ITBS, helps visitors choose a programme, establishes
trust, and provides a direct route to document verification.

The page does not tell the full company story. Institutional history, operating
model, values, and the relationship with the University of Alfred Nobel belong on
the About Us page.

Primary CTA: `Explore programmes`  
Utility action: `Verify a document`

## 12. Removed From Current Prototype

Do not publish until separately confirmed:

- ACCA, EduQual, OTHM, ATHE, QFHE, or generic `EU Partners` logos;
- digital badges and LinkedIn sharing;
- `recognised credentials` as a universal claim;
- measurable organisation results without evidence;
- fictional verification data;
- claims that every programme is international or taught in English;
- claims that every Nobel ITBS document is registered or publicly verifiable.

## 13. Approved Editorial Decisions

- Hero formulation: `Education that moves you forward`.
- Primary public definition: `a European platform for professional education`.
- Legal foundation: the platform operates through the Czech company Nobel ITBS
  s.r.o.
- The University of Alfred Nobel is the exclusive academic partner of Nobel
  ITBS.
- University history and EIT Deep Tech Talent Initiative facts may be used when
  clearly attributed to the University.
