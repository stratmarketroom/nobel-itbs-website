## 4. What Is A Certificate Programme

A Certificate programme is a professional education programme with a defined
subject, learning volume, format, and learning outcomes. After successful
completion, the participant receives the certificate specified by the
individual programme.

The Programme Type does not automatically define the number of ECTS credits,
qualification level, issuer, or international status of the document. These
characteristics are stated separately on each programme page.

## 5. Who This Format Is For

- specialists who want to study a focused professional subject;
- participants who value a defined learning volume;
- professionals who need a document confirming programme completion;
- people choosing a shorter, focused format instead of a long academic
  programme.

## 6. What To Compare

Before choosing a programme, review:

- subject and learning outcomes;
- duration, hours, and ECTS where applicable;
- format and language of instruction;
- assessment and completion requirements;
- name, type, and issuer of the certificate;
- document verification, where provided by the programme.
