# Nobel ITBS Localization Glossary

Product: Nobel ITBS Website and Credential Registry  
Locales: EN, UA, CZ  
Status: working terminology baseline for approved content localization  
Updated: 2026-07-31

## 1. Language And Style

- English uses British spelling and `programme`, not `program`, except inside
  code fields, URLs, or official third-party names.
- Czech and English localizations preserve the factual meaning of the approved
  Ukrainian master copy; they are not independent rewrites.
- Page locale never changes the actual language of instruction.
- Stable slugs and canonical Programme Area names remain unchanged.
- Programme Type and completion document are always translated as separate
  concepts.
- Nobel ITBS, Mini-MBA, ECTS, EQF, Moodle, CPD UK, QR, and Diploma Supplement
  remain unchanged.

## 3. Programme Areas

Canonical public labels in every locale:

- `Business & Management`;
- `Technology & Innovation`;
- `Psychology & Human`.

Localized supporting copy may explain the areas, but must not rename these
canonical labels.

## 4. Programme Names

English canonical names:

- `AI Production`;
- `General Psychology`;
- `Child Psychology`;
- `Neuroplastic Reconstruction`;
- `Space Business`.

Ukrainian and Czech presentation copy may use localized descriptive text, while
the approved public programme title and stable slug remain unchanged unless a
separate naming decision is recorded.

## 7. Legal And Claim Guardrails

- `European professional education platform` is the primary EN positioning.
- The University of Alfred Nobel is the exclusive academic partner of Nobel
  ITBS; other listed organisations are partners, not academic partners.
- Do not translate programme localization into a claim about language of
  instruction.
- Do not translate EQF Level 7 competence alignment as award of an EQF-regulated
  qualification.
- Mini-MBA is not an academic degree or a full MBA.
- Professional development programmes do not automatically grant a profession
  or the right to regulated, medical, psychological, or psychotherapeutic
  practice.
- Partner data never appears in public credential verification.
