## 1. Purpose

This inventory defines the text and materials needed before public-site and programme implementation.

The recommended workflow is:

1. draft EN master copy for shared site pages and UA master copy for programmes;
2. approve structure and claims;
3. adapt shared pages to Ukrainian and Czech and programmes to English and Czech;
4. mark missing translations as fallback-ready;
5. implement structured content fields.

Localization progress:

- UA: all approved non-legal public-page master copies are complete;
- EN: all 17 non-legal public-page localized master copies are complete;
- CZ: all 17 non-legal public-page localized master copies are complete and
  await native-language editorial approval;
- legal pages: approved CZ/EN source documents are available; three UA working
  translations are complete and await Czech counsel approval; all legal pages
  use `noindex, follow`.

## 2. Copy Principles

Tone:

- credible;
- international;
- practical;
- motivating;
- European;
- clear rather than flashy.

Rules:

- programme pages must sell, not describe database records;
- each page needs one clear primary action;
- claims must be supportable by provided proof points;
- do not overpromise accreditation, recognition, employment, or legal credential value;
- no News/Blog copy;
- no public PDF download copy;
- no public name/surname verification copy.
