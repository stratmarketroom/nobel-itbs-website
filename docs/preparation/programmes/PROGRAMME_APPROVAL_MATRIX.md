## 5. Partnership Roles For Neuroplastic Reconstruction

- Nataliia Kholodenko Psychology Centre owns the method/content and delivers the learning experience.
- University of Alfred Nobel provides the academic basis and professional-development certificate.
- Nobel ITBS provides document preparation, certificate-and-supplement packaging, registration, and verification infrastructure.
- MNR consultant status is method-specific and does not equal the regulated profession of psychologist.
- The programme does not authorise psychotherapy or medical practice.
- Partner identity remains programme content and is not copied into public credential verification data.
