## Core Value For Sales Copy

Understand how neuroplasticity relates to emotional regulation and behavioural change, and practise structured, ethically bounded approaches to self-regulation, psychological support, and counselling interaction.

## Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to:

- explain core mechanisms of neuroplasticity and mental regulation;
- connect cognitive, emotional, behavioural, and bodily responses;
- analyse stress, anxiety, and recurring behavioural patterns;
- use basic self-regulation and cognitive-reframing techniques;
- structure a counselling conversation and practise empathic communication;
- analyse crisis-support cases within professional boundaries;
- recognise indications for referral to a psychiatrist or other medical professional;
- reflect on ethics, supervision, competence limits, and professional development.
