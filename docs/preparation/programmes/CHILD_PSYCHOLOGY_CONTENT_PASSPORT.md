## Audience

- psychologists with bachelor or master education in Psychology;
- practical psychologists;
- educators and teachers;
- social and medical workers;
- other professionals who work with children and need child-psychology knowledge.

## Core Value For Sales Copy

Develop an age-sensitive understanding of children's psychological development and deepen professional knowledge of assessment, support, consultation, and interdisciplinary work with children and parents.

## Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to:

- explain psychological development from birth through adolescence;
- analyse age-specific communication, activity, cognition, emotion, and personality development;
- understand school readiness and developmental transitions;
- identify signs of anxiety, depression, trauma-related stress, sleep disturbance, and other risk states within professional limits;
- select valid assessment approaches and evidence-informed support techniques;
- plan psychological interventions and work with children and parents;
- collaborate in mono- and multidisciplinary teams;
- reason through ethical dilemmas and professional boundaries.

## Curriculum

1. General foundations of child psychology, infancy, attachment, early crises, and parenting
2. Preschool and primary-school development, play, projective methods, emotional intelligence, and fatherhood
3. Adolescent and youth development, relationships, separation, risk behaviour, and sexuality education
4. Crisis states, post-traumatic stress, depression, sleep disturbance, and diverse family contexts
5. Counselling children and adolescents, family systems, online counselling, and sexuality education
