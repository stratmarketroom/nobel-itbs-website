## FAQ

### Do I need a psychology background to start?

No. The course is designed as a systematic foundation in general psychology and is suitable for beginners, students who want to fill gaps in foundational topics, and professionals who want to organise their knowledge.

### Can I learn at my own pace?

Yes. The programme is continuously available and has no Zoom sessions or fixed timetable. Access to the Moodle materials is provided for 1 year.

### What is the language of instruction?

The programme is taught in Ukrainian. The English and Czech website versions present this Ukrainian-language programme.

### Does the programme qualify me to work as a psychologist?

No. A standalone programme does not replace a relevant professional education and does not independently grant the right to practise psychology, counselling or psychotherapy.

### What document will I receive?

After completing the programme and final test, you receive a professional development certificate from the University of Alfred Nobel confirming 90 hours / 3 ECTS.
