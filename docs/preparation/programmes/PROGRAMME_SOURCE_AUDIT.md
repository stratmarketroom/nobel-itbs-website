## 4. Resolved Product Decisions

- Space Business is taught in Ukrainian and English.
- AI Production uses `Mini-MBA` as its primary catalogue Programme Type. The full programme is 360 hours / 12 ECTS over 6 months. A University of Alfred Nobel professional development certificate for 180 hours / 6 ECTS is issued after 3 months, followed by a Nobel ITBS international Mini-MBA diploma with Diploma Supplement after the full 6 months. The stated competence level corresponds to EQF Level 7. Programme hours and learning results may be credited toward a full MBA subject to its admission and recognition rules. These public formulations are product-owner approved.
- General Psychology, Child Psychology, and Neuroplastic Reconstruction use `Professional development course` as their confirmed catalogue Programme Type. The approved Ukrainian public category label is `Програма професійного підвищення кваліфікації`; their certificate information is a separate credential attribute.
- Neuroplastic Reconstruction remains the short public name. The long source title is reserved for official programme/document context.
- Neuroplastic Reconstruction is a partner programme. The Nataliia Kholodenko Psychology Centre owns the method/content and delivers the learning experience; the University of Alfred Nobel provides the academic certificate basis; Nobel ITBS provides document preparation, certificate-and-supplement packaging, registration, and verification infrastructure.
- The Neuroplastic Reconstruction public curriculum is confirmed as 12 modules in three stages. A supplement to the University of Alfred Nobel professional development certificate will be issued with the module list and learning volume.
- Neuroplastic Reconstruction starts on 5 October. The confirmed landing tariffs are For Yourself at EUR 990, Master at EUR 1,390, and VIP at EUR 2,090, with the displayed previous prices and tier inclusions preserved. Master and VIP include CPD UK and MNR consultant status; For Yourself does not include certification documents or consultant status.
- Space Business uses `Certificate programme` as its Programme Type and issues a certificate.
- General Psychology, Child Psychology, and Space Business are continuously available distance programmes hosted in Moodle.
- General Psychology and Child Psychology issue a professional development certificate from the University of Alfred Nobel for 90 hours / 3 ECTS. General Psychology provides one-year asynchronous Moodle access; Child Psychology provides six-month asynchronous Moodle access. Neither requires Zoom or a fixed schedule.
- Child Psychology does not include practical classes, placement, internship, clinic practice, or client work. The University of Alfred Nobel Mental Health Clinic is the programme-development base, not a practice site for learners.
- Formal approval, protocol, order, and effective dates from source programmes are not used in public website content.
- Programme Leeloo URLs are intentionally deferred. Old WayForPay links are not migrated; the question/contact fallback applies.

Remaining items include pricing for programmes other than Neuroplastic Reconstruction, enrolment paths, expert and visual assets, and exact wording where the source is incomplete. AI Production credit toward MBA is owner-confirmed; specific admission and recognition conditions will be added when the MBA regulation is ready.

## 5. Claim And Safety Review

- Child Psychology and Neuroplastic Reconstruction contain mental-health, diagnostic, intervention, and crisis-support subject matter. Public copy must avoid implying medical treatment rights or guaranteed clinical outcomes.
- The Neuroplastic programme explicitly states that it does not grant the right to practise psychotherapy or medicine. Preserve this boundary in public FAQ/copy where relevant.
- Neuroplastic Master and VIP include method-specific MNR consultant status and registry entry. Public copy must state that this is not the regulated profession of psychologist and does not authorise psychotherapy or medical practice.
- The university certificate supplement and CPD UK certificate are owner-confirmed for Neuroplastic Master and VIP. Logo-use terms, country-specific practice boundaries, and AI-tool privacy/professional-use safeguards remain to be prepared.
- Child Psychology mentions CPD/BPR points for medical workers. Publish this only after the current eligibility and evidence are confirmed.
- Space Business uses `international-level certificate` and named partner/support claims. Publish only with current approval and evidence.
- Space Business includes minors in its FAQ. Age eligibility, guardian consent, contracting, and privacy handling need legal confirmation before reuse.
- Neuroplastic bibliography contains a URL with a `utm_source=chatgpt.com` parameter. Remove tracking parameters in the controlled academic source; do not reproduce them publicly.
