## Curriculum

`heading`: Eight modules, 90 hours

### 1. Organisation of space activities, 12 hours

Core concepts, the history of the global market, the modern space industry and its key participants.

### 2. Space technologies, 16 hours

The market for goods and services, innovation development, engineering, communication and navigation systems.

### 3. Start-ups in space activities, 16 hours

Commercialisation of scientific programmes, space tourism, resources, space settlements and technology transfer.

### 4. Economics of space activities, 12 hours

Innovation potential, asset management and innovation re-engineering in the space industry.

### 5. International legal framework, 12 hours

The Outer Space Treaty, national regulation and intellectual-property protection.

### 6. International integration and cooperation, 12 hours

Project management, marketing strategy and space-infrastructure logistics.

### 7. Information flows in management, 8 hours

Information processes and management decisions in space-industry companies.

### 8. Consultation on your own project, 2 hours

Structuring the idea and receiving professional feedback on the project.

## FAQ

### Is the programme suitable for beginners?

Yes. It starts with foundational concepts and gradually moves into technologies, economics, law, start-ups and space-project management.

### Can I study at a convenient time?

Yes. This is a continuously available distance programme in Moodle. The access period and schedule of synchronous activities are provided during consultation.

### What are the languages of instruction?

Ukrainian and English. The programme is presented in Czech on the website, but it is not taught in Czech.

### Can I work on my own project?

The programme includes a consultation module for your own project. The precise format and amount of feedback depend on the current participation terms.

### Does the programme guarantee that I will launch a space start-up?

No. It provides knowledge, analytical frameworks and professional context, but does not guarantee investment, partnerships, employment or a commercial outcome.

### What document will I receive?

After meeting the programme requirements, you receive a certificate.
