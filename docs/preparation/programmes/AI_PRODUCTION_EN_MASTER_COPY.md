## FAQ

### Do I need production experience?

Some experience in marketing, sales, education, consulting, content creation or entrepreneurship will be useful. The programme is also suitable for experts planning to create their first structured product.

### Do I need programming skills?

No. The technical component focuses on the applied use of AI, digital platforms and no-code tools.

### Will I create my own product during the programme?

Yes. The practical assignments build a launch portfolio, and the final project brings it together into a complete strategy.

### What is the language of instruction?

The programme is taught in Ukrainian. The English and Czech website versions present the same Ukrainian-language programme.

### What document will I receive?

After the first 3 months, you receive a professional development certificate from the University of Alfred Nobel confirming 180 hours / 6 ECTS. After completing the full six-month programme, you receive an international `Mini-MBA | Professional Development` diploma from Nobel ITBS with a Diploma Supplement. The complete programme has a total scope of 360 hours / 12 ECTS.
