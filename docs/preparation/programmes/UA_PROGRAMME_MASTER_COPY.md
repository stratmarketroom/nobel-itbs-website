## Shared Publishing Rules

- Public programme names remain stable across navigation, catalogue, H1, metadata, and credentials context.
- Formal approval dates from source documents are not used.
- Prices are hidden until confirmed.
- Application URLs are configured separately for Leeloo or the approved partner website.
- Primary fallback CTA: `Поставити запитання`.
- CTA opens the programme question form and stores source programme context.
- Programme page locale does not imply instruction language.
- General Psychology, Child Psychology, and Neuroplastic Reconstruction use the category label `Програма професійного підвищення кваліфікації`.
- Programme Type and the certificate/diploma issued after completion are presented as separate facts.
- Mental-health programme copy must not imply medical treatment rights or guaranteed outcomes.
