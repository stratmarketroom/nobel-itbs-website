## Outcomes

`heading`: After completing the programme, you will be able to

- explain the main patterns of psychological development from birth through adolescence;
- analyse developmental transitions, leading activities and psychological development milestones;
- better understand the development of communication, cognitive processes and emotional life;
- assess psychological readiness for school and adaptation characteristics;
- recognise signs of stress, anxiety and depressive states within the limits of your competence;
- select appropriate methods of psychological assessment and support;
- plan counselling work with children, teenagers and parents;
- collaborate with colleagues in an interdisciplinary team;
- make decisions that respect professional ethics and legal boundaries.

## Curriculum

`heading`: Five content modules

### 1. Foundations of child psychology

Psychological development, psychological age, the perinatal period, early childhood, attachment, basic trust, developmental transitions and parental control.

### 2. Preschool and primary school age

Communication, play, school readiness, projective methods, emotional intelligence, childhood fears, behavioural difficulties and the role of parents.

### 3. Adolescence and young adulthood

Personality development, emotional instability, separation, peer groups, risk-taking behaviour, self-determination and sexuality education.

### 4. Psychological support in crisis situations

Stress responses, traumatic events, anxiety and depressive states, sleep disturbances, risk assessment and the professional boundaries of support.

### 5. Counselling children and teenagers

The family system, counselling interaction, online counselling and age-specific approaches to sensitive topics.

## FAQ

### Is the programme suitable for people other than psychologists?

Yes. It is also designed for educators, social care and healthcare professionals, and others who work with children. Participants should apply their knowledge within the boundaries of their education and professional competence.

### Can I learn at my own pace?

Yes. This is a continuously available Moodle-based distance programme with no Zoom sessions or fixed timetable. Access to the materials is provided for 6 months.

### Does the programme include practical training?

No. This is a theoretical distance-learning programme. It does not include practical classes, placements, training at the Clinic or client work.

### Does the certificate authorise psychotherapy or medical practice?

No. The programme supports professional development but does not replace a foundational professional education or extend the learner's lawful scope of practice.

### What is the language of instruction?

The programme is taught in Ukrainian. The English and Czech pages present the same Ukrainian-language programme.

### What document will I receive?

After completing the programme and final test, you receive a professional development certificate from the University of Alfred Nobel confirming 90 hours / 3 ECTS.
