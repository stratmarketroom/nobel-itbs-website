## Core Value For Sales Copy

Understand the commercial space ecosystem and evaluate opportunities across technology, startups, economics, law, international cooperation, information flows, and project development.

## Learning Outcomes

The existing page supports these cautious outcomes:

- understand the structure and main actors of the global space market;
- recognise major space-technology and service segments;
- explore startup, tourism, resource, settlement, and technology-transfer opportunities;
- understand core economic, legal, intellectual-property, project-management, marketing, and logistics considerations;
- receive guided feedback on a project where the selected package includes it;
- build a network of professional and business contacts.

## Curriculum

1. Organisation of space activities - 12 hours
2. Space technologies - 16 hours
3. Startups in space activities - 16 hours
4. Economics of space activities - 12 hours
5. International legal framework for space activities - 12 hours
6. International integration, resource concentration, and project cooperation - 12 hours
7. Information flows in space-company management - 8 hours
8. Consulting on the learner's project - 2 hours
