## Core Value For Sales Copy

Build a systematic foundation in how psychology explains mental processes, personality, behaviour, motivation, emotion, will, perception, attention, memory, imagination, thinking, intelligence, language, and activity.

This programme is best positioned as a rigorous foundation rather than as a promise of professional clinical qualification.

## Learning Outcomes

Participants should be able to:

- use core psychological concepts and terminology;
- analyse and explain mental processes, states, properties, and behaviour;
- identify psychological problems and reason about possible approaches;
- understand the development and functioning of psychological phenomena;
- find, assess, and synthesize psychological information;
- communicate evidence-based conclusions;
- follow ethical principles and understand professional responsibility.

## Curriculum

1. Psychology as a science
2. Personality and individual psychological differences
3. Psychological analysis of activity
4. Motivation, emotions, and volitional regulation
5. Sensation, perception, and attention
6. Memory and imagination
7. Thinking, intelligence, language, and speech

The syllabus contains 18 detailed topics across these seven blocks.
